ApplyMate: fills job applications for you and tailors your resume to each job
=========================================================================

Install (about 5 minutes, Chrome on Mac or Windows)
1. Unzip this file. Keep the folder somewhere permanent (for example Documents);
   Chrome loads the extension from it, so do not delete or move it later.
2. Open Chrome and go to chrome://extensions
3. Turn on "Developer mode" (top right).
4. Click "Load unpacked" and choose the unzipped "applymate-..." folder.
5. The ApplyMate settings page opens. Fill in:
   - Claude API key: create one at https://console.anthropic.com (you pay Anthropic
     directly; a typical application costs a few cents).
   - Your profile: work through the sections, or upload your resume PDF and click
     "Import profile from resume" to fill most of it in automatically.
   - Your resume PDF, under "Resume".
   - Work authorization and preferences: these answer the visa and logistics questions.
   Click Save.

Use
- Open a job posting, then click Apply. When the application form appears, ApplyMate
  fills it and a panel at the bottom right shows what it filled and what needs you.
- Blue dots mean "check this". Nothing is ever submitted for you.
- If it cannot find the job description it asks you for the posting link.
- Shortcut: Alt+Shift+F fills the current page.
- Settings also has your application tracker (with the exact resume sent to each job,
  exportable to Excel) and a log of every request sent to Claude.

Privacy
- Your profile, resume and answers are stored only in your own Chrome.
- Text is sent to Anthropic's API (with your key) only to answer questions and rewrite
  your resume. You can answer form questions with a free local model instead
  (settings: "Who answers form questions", needs Ollama).

Update
- Replace the folder's contents with a newer version, then click the reload icon on
  the ApplyMate card in chrome://extensions.

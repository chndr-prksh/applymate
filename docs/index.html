ApplyMate

A Chrome extension that fills job applications for you and rewrites your resume for each job. You review everything and press Submit yourself.

Download for Chrome

Free. Works with Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, Workable, iCIMS, Gem and many company career sites. You bring your own Claude API key.

What it does

Fills the formName, contact, location, education, work history, visa questions and custom questions, page by page.
Tailors your resumeReads the job description and rewrites your summary and bullets for it, without inventing anything.
Tracks applicationsEvery job you apply to, with the exact resume you sent. Warns you before applying twice. Exports to Excel.
Stays privateYour profile lives only in your Chrome. Form questions can even be answered by a free local model.

Install (about 5 minutes)

  1. Download the file above and unzip it. Keep the folder somewhere permanent, such as Documents.
  2. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped applymate-… folder.
  4. In the settings page that opens, add a Claude API key from console.anthropic.com, upload your resume and fill in your profile. Click Save.
  5. Open a job, click Apply, and watch the panel in the bottom-right corner.

Chrome shows a "Developer mode" notice because ApplyMate is not from the Chrome Web Store. A typical application costs a few cents in Claude usage, billed to your own key.

Updating

Download the new version, replace the folder's contents, and click the reload icon on the ApplyMate card in chrome://extensions.
